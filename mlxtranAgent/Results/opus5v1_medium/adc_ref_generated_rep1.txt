DESCRIPTION:
ADC PK model with 2-compartment disposition of the conjugate (ADC) and
release of the cytotoxic payload via the proteolytic (catabolic) pathway only.
The released payload follows a one-compartment disposition.
ADC PK is written with PK macros, payload PK with ODEs.

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {k12, k21, V, k10, V_payload, k_payload}

PK:
; drug-to-antibody ratio (constant)
DAR = 4

; --- ADC disposition (macros) ---
compartment(cmt=1, amount=Ac, volume=V, concentration=Cc_ADC)
iv(cmt=1, adm=1)
peripheral(k12, k21)
elimination(cmt=1, k=k10)

EQUATION:
; --- Released payload (ODE), initial amount = 0 ---
A_payload_0 = 0
ddt_A_payload = DAR*k10*Ac - k_payload*A_payload
Cc_payload = A_payload/V_payload

OUTPUT:
output = {Cc_ADC, Cc_payload}
