DESCRIPTION:
Antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) model.
ADC: 2-compartment disposition with IV administration (macros).
Payload: released in the central compartment via proteolytic catabolism of the ADC
(DAR molecules of payload per ADC molecule), 1-compartment disposition (ODEs).

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {V, k10, k12, k21, V_payload, k_payload}

PK:
; --- ADC disposition written with macros ---
compartment(cmt=1, amount=Ac_ADC, volume=V, concentration=Cc_ADC)
iv(cmt=1, adm=1)
peripheral(k12, k21)
elimination(cmt=1, k=k10)

EQUATION:
odeType = stiff

; Constant drug-to-antibody ratio
DAR = 4

; --- Payload disposition written with ODEs ---
A_payload_0 = 0
Cc_payload = A_payload / V_payload
ddt_A_payload = DAR * k10 * Ac_ADC - k_payload * A_payload

OUTPUT:
output = {Cc_ADC, Cc_payload}
