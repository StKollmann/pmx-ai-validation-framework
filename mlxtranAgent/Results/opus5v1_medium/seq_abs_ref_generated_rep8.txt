DESCRIPTION:
Two-compartment PK model with sequential zero-order then first-order absorption.
Dose enters a depot compartment (cmt=1) via a zero-order process of duration Tk0,
then is transferred to the central compartment (cmt=2) by a first-order rate ka.

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {Tk0, ka, V, Cl, Q, V2}

PK:
; transfer rate constants between central (2) and peripheral (3) compartments
k23 = Q/V
k32 = Q/V2

; depot compartment with zero-order input
compartment(cmt=1, amount=Ad)
absorption(cmt=1, Tk0)

; central compartment
compartment(cmt=2, amount=Ac, volume=V, concentration=Cc)

; first-order transfer from depot to central
transfer(from=1, to=2, kt=ka)

; peripheral compartment and linear elimination from central
peripheral(k23, k32)
elimination(cmt=2, Cl)

OUTPUT:
output = {Cc}
