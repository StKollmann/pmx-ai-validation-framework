DESCRIPTION:
ADC PK model: 2-compartment ADC disposition (IV) with proteolytic release of cytotoxic payload
from the central compartment (constant DAR = 4). Payload follows 1-compartment disposition.
ADC part written with PK macros, payload part with ODEs.

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {k12, k21, V, k10, V_payload, k_payload}

PK:
; --- ADC disposition (macros) ---
compartment(cmt=1, amount=Ac, volume=V, concentration=Cc_ADC)
peripheral(k12, k21)
iv(cmt=1, adm=1)
elimination(cmt=1, k=k10)

EQUATION:
; --- constant drug-to-antibody ratio ---
DAR = 4

; --- initial conditions ---
t_0 = 0
A_payload_0 = 0

; --- released payload (proteolytic pathway only) ---
ddt_A_payload = DAR*k10*Ac - k_payload*A_payload

Cc_payload = A_payload/V_payload

OUTPUT:
output = {Cc_ADC, Cc_payload}
