DESCRIPTION:
Target cell limited model of viral dynamics.
Target cells (T) are infected by virus (V) at rate beta, becoming infected
cells (I) which die at rate delta and produce virus at rate p.
Virus is cleared at rate c. Output is the log10 viral load, saturated below at -5.

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {beta, delta, p, c, V0, T0}

EQUATION:
odeType = stiff
t_0 = 0
T_0 = T0
I_0 = 0
V_0 = V0

ddt_T = -beta*T*V
ddt_I = beta*T*V - delta*I
ddt_V = p*I - c*V

Vpos = max(V, 1e-10)
LV = max(log10(Vpos), -5)

OUTPUT:
output = {LV}
table = {T, I, V}
