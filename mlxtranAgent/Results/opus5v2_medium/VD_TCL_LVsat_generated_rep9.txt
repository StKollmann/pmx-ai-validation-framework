[LONGITUDINAL]
DESCRIPTION:
Target cell limited model of viral dynamics.
Target cells T are infected by virus V at rate beta, becoming infected
cells I which die at rate delta and produce virus at rate p.
Virus is cleared at rate c. Output is the log10 viral load LV,
saturated below at -5.

input = {beta, delta, p, c, V0, T0}

EQUATION:
odeType = stiff
t_0 = 0
T_0 = T0
I_0 = 0
V_0 = V0

ddt_T = -beta*T*V
ddt_I = beta*T*V - delta*I
ddt_V = p*I - c*V

; log10 viral load with lower saturation at -5
Vpos = max(V, 1e-5)
LV = log10(Vpos)

OUTPUT:
output = {LV}
table = {T, I, V}
