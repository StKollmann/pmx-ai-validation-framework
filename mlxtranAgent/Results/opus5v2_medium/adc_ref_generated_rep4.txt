DESCRIPTION:
Antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) model.
ADC: 2-compartment disposition with IV administration (macros).
Payload: released from the ADC in the central compartment through the
proteolytic (catabolism) pathway only, with a constant DAR of 4,
then follows a 1-compartment disposition (ODEs).

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {k12, k21, V, k10, V_payload, k_payload}

PK:
; --- ADC disposition (macros) ---
compartment(cmt=1, amount=Ac, volume=V, concentration=Cc_ADC)
iv(cmt=1, adm=1)
peripheral(k12, k21)
elimination(cmt=1, k=k10)

EQUATION:
; --- constant drug-to-antibody ratio ---
DAR = 4

; --- payload disposition (ODE) ---
t_0 = 0
Ap_0 = 0
ddt_Ap = DAR*k10*Ac - k_payload*Ap

Cc_payload = Ap/V_payload

OUTPUT:
output = {Cc_ADC, Cc_payload}
